A master's thesis on social media radio as an epistemic platform: Epistemic Systems Theory, the LOKLOK design, a measured student wave, a calibrated in-silico experiment, a randomized-assignment triangulation, and an honest null result in machine coding. LOKLOK: does an epistemic-first radio design improve how university students judge what they hear, without losing the audience?
