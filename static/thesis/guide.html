How to read this thesis

The thesis is a self-contained research archive: raw questionnaires, the reconstructed analysis matrix, the FGD and self-report transcripts, the corpora, the code, the seeds, the outputs, and the figures. It maintains a strict separation between measured field evidence, model-based counterfactual evidence, and theoretical propositions.

The short path (about one hour)

  1. Abstract (p. ix): the whole argument in 400 words.
  2. Section 1.2.3 (p. 42): why the case is radio-specific, not generic social media.
  3. Table 6.26 and Section 6.12.15 (p. 246): the human-gold validation and the honest null result.
  4. Table 6.27 and Section 6.12.17 (p. 250): the randomized-assignment triangulation.
  5. Section 6.14 (p. 255): the trade-off the thesis foregrounds.
  6. Chapter 7 (p. 256): the bounded conclusions.

The deep Chapter 6 subsections are kept in place so that numeric and definitional links remain adjacent; the reader can follow the short path without losing the chain of evidence.

Claim tiers

Tier 1

Measured

What the 52 respondents reported. Descriptive associations only; no causal wording.

Tier 2

Model-conditional

What the specified simulation implies, given declared weights. Always labelled as such.

Tier 3

Theoretical

What EST proposes, stated as falsifiable propositions rather than findings.

Tier 4

Prospective

The planned field experiment and LLM-agent study: specifications, not results.
