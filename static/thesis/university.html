The institution

The university behind the thesis

The LOKLOK thesis was prepared within the supervision relationship of the Faculty of Mass Communication, Ahram Canadian University. This page presents the institution, its Dean's office, the candidate, and the two supervisors, with the same honesty rules as the thesis: named people and roles only as recorded in the submitted front matter.

Office of the Dean

Prof. Inas Abou Youssef

Dean of the Faculty of Mass Communication and Acting Dean of the School of Business Administration, Ahram Canadian University; Professor of Journalism.
Universities build the media their graduates will run. A thesis about how platform architecture shapes what students hear, and what they become able to judge, belongs precisely to this Faculty's mission: media innovation in the public interest.

The candidate acknowledges Prof. Inas Abou Youssef with gratitude for her patience and effort in making this thesis a reality, and for leading the Faculty under whose supervision it was completed.
