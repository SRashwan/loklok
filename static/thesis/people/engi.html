Dr. Engi Aboul-Ezz

The first supervisor of the LOKLOK thesis. Dr. Engi Aboul-Ezz guided the framing of the research problem, the development of the radio-specific scope, and the theoretical grounding of Epistemic Systems Theory, with sustained feedback across every draft of the manuscript.

The candidate acknowledges Dr. Engi Aboul-Ezz with gratitude for her supervision, patience, and the careful readings that shaped the thesis into its final form.
