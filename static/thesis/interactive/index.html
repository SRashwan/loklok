The interactive laboratory

Each instrument reproduces a piece of the thesis's arithmetic in the browser. They are simplified linearisations of the declared model, stated as such on every page; the exact numbers always come from the 18-session simulation reported in the thesis.

IThe trade-off explorer

Move the declared state-outcome weights and watch the epistemic gain and the engagement cost move together across a sampled Pareto frontier, with the 256-draw sweep band drawn for reference.

Open applet

IIThe BRS diffusion simulator

A seven-agent network runs the reputation update live. Switch the BRS path on and off, and switch between within-persona and between-agent assignment to see why the effect is interference-sensitive (+0.190 versus +0.038).

Open applet

IIIThe eight cells

The full 2^3 factorial as an explorable chart: cell means, main effects, interactions, and the contrast under four assignment designs.

Open applet

IVFeynman diagrams

The ten experimental processes in particle-physics notation. Click any line or vertex to read exactly what it stands for.

Open applet

VThe codebook examiner

All 25 qualitative codes with human agreement and machine performance, checked against the pre-set usability gate. Sort, filter, and read the honest conclusion.

Open applet
Every applet labels its inputs as declared assumptions and its outputs as illustrative. Nothing here is a human treatment effect.
