The interactive laboratory

Live implementations of the thesis's algorithms and statistics: rank a real feed, run the rule-based simulation, watch the inference happen, and explore every figure. Each instrument labels its inputs as declared assumptions and its outputs as illustrative; the exact numbers always come from the 18-session simulation reported in the thesis.

IThe trade-off explorer

Move the declared state-outcome weights and watch the epistemic gain and the engagement cost move together across a sampled Pareto frontier, with the 256-draw sweep band for reference.

Open applet

IIThe BRS diffusion simulator

A seven-agent network runs the reputation update live. Switch the BRS path and the assignment design to see why the effect is interference-sensitive (+0.190 versus +0.038).

Open applet

IIIThe eight cells, explored

Hover every cell for its exact value, click to pin its composition, and switch to the assignment-designs view with hoverable whiskers.

Open applet

IVFeynman diagrams

The ten experimental processes in particle-physics notation, collision-free, with every line and vertex explained on click.

Open applet

VThe codebook examiner

All 25 qualitative codes with human agreement and machine performance, checked against the pre-set usability gate. Sort, filter, and read the honest conclusion.

Open applet

VIA real LOKLOK home feed

Twelve radio items, ranked twice: engagement-first and epistemic-first (BRS + MORE diversity + ERIC moderation + echo cooling). Move the listener and watch both feeds change, with a live comparison of diversity, echo, credibility, and engagement.

Open applet

VIIThe pipeline simulator

Run the rule-based ABM yourself: personas, sessions, DeGroot reputation diffusion, diversity choice, cooling, moderation. Animate G0 against G1 session by session and reproduce the ablation pattern (B carries the contrast).

Open applet

VIIIBayesian vs frequentist

Watch the thesis headline +0.827 [0.779, 0.875] accumulate persona by persona: the shrinking confidence interval, the updating posterior, and P(effect > 0), with three priors to try.

Open applet

IXInteractive figures

Live twins of the key figures: the sweep histogram with a draggable threshold, the Pareto scatter with hover, the triangulation forest, and the rating distributions.
