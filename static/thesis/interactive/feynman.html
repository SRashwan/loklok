Pedagogical notation borrowed from particle physics; the diagrams are supplements, not physics claims. Line conventions: solid arrow = agent or content line; wiggly = audio signal, cooling, prompt, or response; curly = platform mechanism exchange; dashed = misinformation and corrected content; blob = algorithmic filter; cross = randomization or measurement point.
