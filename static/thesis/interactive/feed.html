What the two feeds do to the listener

Simplified declared scoring: engagement = 0.74 + 0.35(echo - .30) - 0.10(chosen_dbi - 1) + 0.08(quality - .5); credibility = -.25 + 1.2(BRS - .5) + .55 x diversity + .45(quality - .5) + .80(scrutiny - .5), minus a moderation penalty for misinformation items when ERIC is on. Exact numbers come from the 18-session simulation.

The point of the experiment: the engagement-first feed maximizes what people stay for; the epistemic-first feed trades a little engagement for credibility and viewpoint spread. That is the trade-off the thesis measures at +0.827 versus -0.183.
