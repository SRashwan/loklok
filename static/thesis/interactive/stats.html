Watch the inference happen

The thesis headline is a paired difference over 500 generated personas: mean +0.827, interval [0.779, 0.875]. This simulator draws those personas one by one and shows, live, what the frequentist confidence interval and the Bayesian posterior each claim as n grows, and why the thesis warns that this interval is persona variation, not human sampling error.

The numbers are the thesis's own: each persona contributes a difference of about +0.827 with SD 0.0245. The frequentist 95% interval and the Bayesian 95% credible interval nearly coincide here because both summarize the same simulated differences; neither one is independent evidence about humans.
