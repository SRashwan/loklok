The BRS diffusion simulator

Seven agents update their broadcaster reputation score by mixing their neighbours' opinions with a personal anchor. The anchor is the declared BRS architecture (factor A). Watch what happens to the mean when every agent carries A, and when assignment is randomized between agents: this is why the A effect is interference-sensitive.
