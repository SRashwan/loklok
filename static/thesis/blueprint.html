The Epistemic Platform Blueprint

How to build a platform that raises what people are able to judge, not just what they click. This page converts Epistemic Systems Theory, the LOKLOK mechanisms, and the calibrated-experiment findings into a replicable recipe for researchers, developers, and platform teams. Every number links back to the thesis; the interactive implementations are in the laboratory.

1. Six design principles

P1 Anchor reputation. Every source carries a reputation score updated by evidence (BRS). Without an anchor, the feed optimizes noise.
P2 Constrain diversity. Rank by trust plus viewpoint spread (MORE). Diversity is a hard constraint, not a decoration: B carries the contrast (+0.6265).
P3 Measure with accountability. Publish reliability and validity separately; never claim structure the data did not show.
P4 Close the participation loop. Moderation reflects and invites correction (ERIC); corrections have a half-life, so re-anchor quickly (the gate).
P5 Evaluate assignment-robustly. A claim must survive four assignment designs and placebo controls; the BRS path did not, and that is a finding, not a failure.
P6 Report the null. The qualitative gate was not met, and the thesis says so. A pre-set rule you keep is worth more than a threshold you moved.

3. The home feed algorithm, step by step

The recipe the thesis's feed applet implements (try it live):

for each candidate item i: sim_i = 1 - |angle_i - viewpoint_user| # similarity to the listener dbi_i = |angle_i - viewpoint_user| # diversity distance echo_i = sim_i * echo_susceptibility_user # echo exposure if cooling_on and echo_i >= theta_c: echo_i *= 0.76 uvs_i = mean(BRS) * (1 - prop_i * kappa) * (1 - m_i) # slot value m_i = max(0, s * (prop_i - tau_mod)) # ERIC penalty L_i = w1 * uvs_i + w2 * dbi_i + w4 * quality_i - mu * sai_i+ # MORE objective rank candidates by L_i (epistemic-first) or by engagement_i (engagement-first)
$$L_i = w_1\,\mathrm{uvs}_i + w_2\,\mathrm{dbi}_i + w_4\,q_i - \mu\,\mathrm{sai}_i^{+}$$

Parameter defaults and what to tune (full derivations on the Algorithms page):

ParameterThesis defaultTuning guidance
w1 (reputation)1.0Raise for trust-heavy markets (news, health).
w2 (diversity)0.9Raise for polarized audiences; this is the contrast engine.
mu (salience penalty)0.9Raise to suppress outrage bait; threshold 0.22.
s, tau_mod (ERIC)1.4 / 0.3Softer for satire-heavy catalogs; steeper for safety-critical ones.
theta_c (cooling)0.3Lower it to cool echo earlier; cooling multiplies echo by 0.76.
tau (attention gate)declaredEstimate it from real telemetry before believing any retention number.

4. Rankings and reputation

$$b_i^{(t+1)} = (1-\lambda)\sum_j \tilde A_{ij} b_j^{(t)} + \lambda\, a_i, \qquad a_i = 0.55\,c_i + 0.25\,g + 0.20\,\bar q + 0.20$$
  • Reputation is a network consensus with an evidence anchor. The anchor keeps the crowd honest; lambda = 0.35 when the BRS path is on.
  • Never rank on reputation alone. The triangulation showed the reputation path is interference-sensitive (+0.190 within-persona, +0.038 between agents); pair it with the diversity objective, which is assignment-robust.
  • Cold start: anchor new broadcasters at the quality expectation q_bar and let evidence move them; do not inherit the previous owner's score.
  • Update gates: corrections decay like exp(-dt/tau); schedule re-anchoring within the coherence window, not next week.

5.2 How scoring is done

  • SR eval: the 1-7 evaluative rating, computed as the model's latent credibility trajectory mapped onto the rating scale; G0 anchored to the measured PB mean (5.119).
  • SR comp: the NMLS 35-item profile scored with the Samejima graded response model \(P(x \ge k \mid \theta) = \frac{1}{1+\exp[-a(\theta-b_k)]}\) then rescaled to the 0-100 display, without redefining the published instrument.
  • Engagement: the declared state-outcome mapping \(\mathrm{eng} = 0.74 + 0.35(e-.30) - 0.10(\mathrm{dbi}-1) + 0.08(q-.5)\) reported both as the session average (-0.183) and the terminal measure (-0.217).
  • Psychometric accountability (EST-P3): reliability coefficients are computed in the model (alpha, omega, Guttman lambda6, split-half, test-retest ICC = .953); these are properties of the generating model, not human validation.

For production platforms, the thesis's psychometric guidance: report alpha and omega (McNeish, 2018; Flora, 2020), use dynamic fit-index cutoffs for CFA (McNeish & Wolf, 2023), test invariance before comparing groups (Putnick & Bornstein, 2016), guard against common method bias (Podsakoff et al., 2024), and follow the AERA/APA/NCME Standards (2014) and APA JARS (Appelbaum et al., 2018; Levitt et al., 2018). 6. EMA: the Ecological Momentary Assessment, dedicated section

Yes, the thesis specifies EMA, and it is stated clearly (Chapter 5, Section "The Ecological Momentary Assessment Schedule", p. 125 of the PDF; symbol list entry "EMA"; and the Zewail gate figure caption, where "the EMA probe is the delayed measurement"). Its status, in one honest paragraph:

What the thesis specifies. Four measurement windows: pre-broadcast (PR1-PR3), post-broadcast (PB1-PB5 plus the star rating), echo-triggered (EM1-EM3), and weekly (WK1-WK15). The intended post-broadcast trigger is within 30 seconds of an episode ending, and the echo trigger is linked to the platform's diversity indicator. These timing rules describe the intended EMA implementation. What the thesis does not claim. The supplied cross-sectional exports contain no session timestamps, so compliance with the 30-second window cannot be assessed; the results are post-broadcast questionnaire results, not demonstrated longitudinal or real-time behavioural effects. In the ABM, EMA is not a running measurement loop: the Zewail gate uses the EMA probe as the delayed measurement in its pump-probe analogy, and the 18-session schedule is a simulation design, not an EMA schedule. The field wave anchors the model level; the EMA windows are the specification a real deployment must implement.

EMA implementation guide for platforms. (1) Instrument every trigger with a server timestamp and measure compliance as the share of probes answered inside the window; (2) keep post-broadcast probes to the five PB items plus the star so the window stays under a minute; (3) tie echo triggers to the same diversity indicator that drives cooling, so measurement and intervention share one definition; (4) store pre/post pairs per episode to enable real within-person change estimation later; (5) treat EMA as the field twin of the simulation's session measurements: 18 simulated sessions is a schedule, EMA is the human version of it.

7. Evaluation before launch: the readiness ladder

Step 1: Specify. Write the ranking objective, the reputation update, and the measurement model as equations before any code ships (Chapters 3-4).
Step 2: Calibrate in silico. Anchor personas to a measured wave (DML + IPW debiasing, spectral manifold), declare every weight, and report the sweep (Section 6.10).
Step 3: Triangulate assignment. Run within-persona, agent-randomized, design-based, and cluster-randomized designs; expect the reputation path to weaken between agents (Section 6.12.17).
Step 4: Run controls. Placebo factors, negative controls, mechanism checks, topology checks, and adversarial content stress (Section 6.12).
Step 5: Pilot the agent formalism. A single-shot LLM appraisal reproduces direction but not magnitude or engagement sign (Section 6.13); treat it as triangulation, not validation.
Step 6: Field with frozen allocations. The prospective platform experiment: frozen treatment allocations, disclosed LLM-agent logs, EMA windows implemented, and a pre-set stop rule (Chapter 5, plan).

8. Playbooks by role

For researchers

  • Report alpha and omega, never alpha alone.
  • State the CFA fit even when it is poor; do not move the usability gate.
  • Label intervals: persona variation, not human sampling error.
  • Pre-register the machine-coding gate and the four assignment designs.
  • Estimate invariance before any cross-group latent comparison.

For developers

  • Implement the ranking objective as a pure function of declared parameters.
  • Log every trigger timestamp (EMA compliance is a metric, not a promise).
  • Keep the gate, cooling, and ERIC as switchable components for ablation.
  • Version the persona prior and the weights together with the model artifact.
  • Serve the trade-off dashboard: epistemic gain vs engagement cost, live.

For platform and business teams

  • Adopt a two-number KPI: rating quality up (+0.827) and engagement cost (-0.183), never engagement alone.
  • Expect the reputation feature to underperform in noisy networks; the diversity objective is the robust asset.
  • Correct fast: attention decays with the dephasing time, so re-anchor within the window.
  • Budget for the honest null: some machine-coding goals will fail, and publishing that builds trust.

9. Anti-patterns to avoid

Engagement-only ranking. The engagement-first feed concentrates echo; the thesis measured the cost of the alternative and chose to report it.
Moving the gate after the fact. The usability rule (8 positives, kappa >= .60, <= 5% fabrication) was kept when it failed. A threshold you move is not a test.
Claiming structural validity from reliability. High alpha with a poor CFA is a mixed result, reported as mixed.
Treating simulated intervals as human confidence. They describe generated personas; the human causal claim remains future work.
