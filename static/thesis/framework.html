The theoretical and conceptual framework

Every variable in this thesis has a declared type, an operationalization, and a source. This page states them all: the theoretical lens (EST), the two conceptual models (the descriptive field wave and the causal-in-the-model experiment), the variable ledger with independent variables, dependent variables, mediators, moderators, and controls, and the measurement model behind every score.

1. The theoretical framework: EST

The thesis theorizes through Epistemic Systems Theory, four falsifiable propositions that link platform architecture to audience judgment. EST is the lens; LOKLOK is its specification; the experiment is its test.

PropositionTheoretical claimTested by
EST-P1Architecture is a structural condition of credibilityFactor A (BRS) in the ABM; PB-star associations in the field wave
EST-P2Diversity and cooling constrain epistemic concentrationFactor B (MORE + cooling); the dominant path in the decomposition
EST-P3Measurement is an accountability requirementReliability, CFA, the machine-coding gate; reported separately from claims
EST-P4Participation and evidence-based production are the educational testReserved for the prospective field experiment (H7)

2. The conceptual framework in one paragraph

Platform components (A: reputation architecture, B: diversity and cooling, C: moderation) act on the listener's states (reputation beliefs, chosen diversity, echo, attention), and those states produce the observable ratings (evaluative and competence) and engagement. Persona traits and the declared weights shape how strongly each component transmits; the whole model is anchored by a measured wave and audited by controls. The field wave is descriptive: it fixes the level and spread of the anchors, and no causal wording is attached to it.

6. The measurement model

Both simulated rating formats are generated from latent constructs through the Samejima graded response model; the field instruments are the published batteries scored as designed.

$$P(x \ge k \mid \theta) = \frac{1}{1+\exp[-a(\theta-b_k)]}$$
  • Latent construct: media literacy theta (simulated) behind the NMLS profile; evaluative credibility behind SR eval; engagement behind the declared mapping.
  • Indicators: NMLS 35 items across four domains (functional and critical consumption and prosumption); MCS + PB 8 items; BRS 11 items; PB 5 items.
  • Model-fit stance: reliability is reported (alpha and omega); structural validity is not claimed (poor CFA); invariance is not estimated. Full ledger on the Quality page.
