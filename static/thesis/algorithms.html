The algorithms, equation by equation

Every mechanism in the thesis is a declared equation, not a black box. This page is a working reference: each section states the equation, breaks it down term by term, gives its physical and social meaning, and then lets you tweak the parameters and watch the equation run. The values used in the calibrated experiment are marked as the defaults.

1. The Broadcaster Reputation Score (BRS)

$$b_i^{(t+1)} \;=\; (1-\lambda)\,\sum_{j} \tilde{A}_{ij}\, b_j^{(t)} \;+\; \lambda\, a_i, \qquad a_i \;=\; 0.55\, c_i + 0.25\, g + 0.20\, \bar q + 0.20$$
b_i reputation of agent i at session t.
Σ Ã_ij b_j DeGroot averaging over the attention network.
λ anchoring weight: 0.35 with the BRS path on, 0.15 off.
a_i personal anchor from scrutiny, the gate, and quality expectation.

Physical meaning. A consensus protocol with a drift term: the network diffuses belief, the anchor pins it to evidence. Social meaning. A broadcaster's reputation is what other listeners believe, tempered by what each listener can check. Context. The BRS is architecture component A; the triangulation showed its effect is interference-sensitive (+0.190 within personas, +0.038 between agents).

RunReset
Press Run: every agent updates b_i from its neighbours plus its own anchor. A high lambda makes each listener trust their own check; a low lambda makes the crowd dominate.

2. MORE: the diversity-constrained recommendation

$$L_i \;=\; w_1\,\mathrm{uvs}_i \;+\; w_2\,\mathrm{dbi}_i \;-\; \mu\,\mathrm{sai}_i^{+}, \qquad \mathrm{uvs}_i = \bar b\,(1 - p_i^{\mathrm{prop}}\kappa)(1 - m_i), \qquad \mathrm{dbi}_i = 1 - h\,\langle \hat r_i, \hat c\rangle$$
L_i the score a candidate is ranked by; the feed picks arg max.
uvs_i slot value: mean reputation, discounted by propaganda share and moderation.
dbi_i diversity benefit: 1 minus the Hilbert overlap with the listener.
sai_i⁺ salience penalty beyond 0.22, weight μ.

Physical meaning. Constrained optimization written as a penalized objective. Social meaning. The feed rewards trusted broadcasters but pays extra for viewpoints outside the bubble; that is why B carries the contrast (+0.6265, PRCC +0.77 for the diversity-to-credibility link).

Raise w2 and diverse, trusted items jump the queue; raise w1 and popularity takes over. The thesis default balances both, which is the whole point of MORE.

3. ERIC: reflect, invite, correct

$$m_i \;=\; \max\big(0,\; s\,(p_i^{\mathrm{prop}} - \tau_{\mathrm{mod}})\big)$$

Breakdown. Zero below the moderation threshold, linear beyond it (slope s = 1.4 in the model). The penalty enters the slot value and the credibility mapping. Physical meaning. A soft gate. Social meaning. Moderation as an interaction, not deletion: flagged items stay visible, rank lower, and carry a reflection prompt. Context. C alone is near zero (-0.0121); it matters through the B x C interaction (+0.3791).

Drag p or the curve's knobs: the penalty is zero until p crosses tau, then grows linearly. Items at p = 0.2, 0.5, 0.8 are marked as examples.

4. Echo Meter and Algorithmic Cooling

$$e^{(t+1)} = 0.75\, e^{(t)} + 0.25\,(1-\mathrm{dbi}^{(t)}), \qquad e \leftarrow 0.76\, e \;\text{ if }\; e \ge \theta_{\mathrm{cool}}$$

Breakdown. Echo accumulates toward sameness; cooling multiplies by 0.76 once echo crosses the threshold. Physical meaning. Relaxation with active cooling. Social meaning. The platform notices when you have been inside one bubble for a while and gently widens the mix. Context. The mechanism check confirmed B x C lowers echo by -0.340 (SD .009).

cooling on Run
Start from a hot bubble (echo 0.85). Without cooling the listener stays inside; with cooling each crossing of theta_c shaves 24% off echo.

5. The Zewail-inspired attention gate

$$R \;=\; \exp\!\big(-\,\Delta t \,/\, \tau\big)$$

Breakdown. R is the fraction of an evidence anchor retained after a delay Δt, with dephasing time τ. Physical meaning. Time-resolved coherence, as in a pump-probe experiment. Social meaning. Corrections have a half-life: if the platform does not re-anchor quickly, the correction decays. Context. The gate is open about 15% of the time; the dephasing time is a declared assumption, and the thesis says so.

Animate
Move the marker along the decay: after one tau only 37% of the correction remains; after three tau almost nothing. Fast re-anchoring is the design answer.

6. GRM-IRT scoring

$$P(x \ge k \mid \theta) \;=\; \frac{1}{1 + \exp\!\big[-a\,(\theta - b_k)\big]}$$

Breakdown. θ latent trait, b_k level difficulty, a discrimination. Physical meaning. A soft threshold crossing. Social meaning. Ratings are scored against item difficulties, so a competence rating is a position on a latent scale, not a count of clicks. Context. Synthetic batteries: NMLS alpha .948, MCS alpha .738, ICC .953; properties of the generating model, not human validation.

Sweep theta
The curves are P(x at least k) for levels k = 1, 2, 3. At your theta the bars show the response probabilities; the expected score moves smoothly with ability.

7. DML and IPW debiasing

$$\hat\theta_{\mathrm{DML}} = \frac{\sum_i (Y_i - \hat m(X_i))(Z_i - \hat e(X_i))}{\sum_i (Z_i - \hat e(X_i))^2}, \qquad w_i = \frac{Z_i}{\hat e(X_i)} + \frac{1-Z_i}{1-\hat e(X_i)}$$

Breakdown. DML partials out the nuisance functions before comparing arms; IPW reweights by inverse treatment probability. Physical meaning. Orthogonalization. Social meaning. The 52 measured students were not randomly assigned; these procedures make the comparison as balanced as the observed covariates allow, and the residual imbalance (0.127) is reported, not hidden.

Resample data
True ATE = 2.0. The naive difference is biased by the confounder; DML removes the predictable part and IPW reweights, both recovering about 2.0. This is how the measured wave becomes the persona prior.

Breakdown. The ablation showed three are outcome-active: DeGroot (-0.233), Lagrange (-0.750), Hilbert (-0.453) when off. Canonical dynamics chained together. Social meaning. Each filter is one named social process: diffusion, conformity, contraction, energy, similarity, percolation, choice, cooling.

9. The joint sweep and PRCC sensitivity

$$r_{x_j} = \frac{\mathrm{Cov}(x_j, y \mid x_{-j})}{\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}(x_j \mid x_{-j})\,\mathrm{Var}(y \mid x_{-j})}}$$

Breakdown. 256 draws vary six mechanism parameters and five state-outcome weights; the partial rank correlation of each input with the contrast, given the others, is reported. Top values: diversity-to-credibility +0.77, BRS-to-credibility +0.31, echo-to-credibility -0.27. Social meaning. One declared assumption, the link between exposure diversity and perceived credibility, carries most of the result; the thesis names it rather than hiding it.

Hover a bar for its exact partial rank correlation. Raising the declared diversity-to-credibility weight moves the whole sweep upward; at zero the positive share collapses.

10. The four assignment designs

$$\widehat{ATE} = \frac{1}{n_1}\sum_{i: Z_i=1} Y_i - \frac{1}{n_0}\sum_{i: Z_i=0} Y_i$$

Breakdown. The same estimator under four assignment rules: within-persona, per-agent randomization across 40 worlds, design-based draws from the potential-outcome matrix (400), and cluster randomization (20). The design-based draw contains no interference, so comparing it with the agent-randomized design isolates interference. Social meaning. A treatment effect that survives every assignment rule is a property of the design; one that moves with assignment is a property of the network. B survives; A does not.

Run 400 worlds
The toy effect is ATE = 2.0. Without interference all designs recover it; with spillover, the agent-randomized design dilutes the estimate exactly as the BRS path does in the thesis.
