The algorithms, equation by equation

Every mechanism in the thesis is a declared equation, not a black box. This page states each one, breaks it down term by term, and gives its physical meaning (the borrowed formal metaphor), its social meaning (what it does to a listener), and its context (the parameter values used in the calibrated experiment). The interactive versions live in the laboratory.

1. The Broadcaster Reputation Score (BRS)

$$b_i^{(t+1)} \;=\; (1-\lambda)\,\sum_{j} \tilde{A}_{ij}\, b_j^{(t)} \;+\; \lambda\, a_i, \qquad a_i \;=\; 0.55\, c_i + 0.25\, g + 0.20\, \bar q + 0.20$$
b_i reputation of agent i at session t; updated once per session.
Σ Ã_ij b_j DeGroot averaging over the attention network: listeners adopt what their network believes.
λ anchoring weight: 0.35 when the BRS path (A) is on, 0.15 when it is off.
a_i personal anchor: 0.55 from critical scrutiny c_i, 0.25 from the gate g, 0.20 from quality expectation q̄, plus 0.20 base.

Physical meaning. A consensus protocol with a drift term: the network term diffuses belief, the anchor term pins it to evidence.

Social meaning. A broadcaster's reputation is what other listeners believe about them, tempered by what each listener can check for themselves.

Context. The BRS is the architecture component A. The triangulation showed its effect is interference-sensitive: +0.190 within personas, +0.038 between agents.

2. MORE: the diversity-constrained recommendation

$$L_i \;=\; w_1\,\mathrm{uvs}_i \;+\; w_2\,\mathrm{dbi}_i \;-\; \mu\,\mathrm{sai}_i^{+}, \qquad \mathrm{sai}_i^{+}=\max\big(0,\;|\mathrm{sai}_i|-0.22\big)$$
$$\mathrm{uvs}_i \;=\; \bar b \;\big(1 - p_i^{\mathrm{prop}}\,\kappa\big)\;\big(1 - m_i\big), \qquad \mathrm{dbi}_i \;=\; 1 - h\,\big\langle \hat r_i,\; \hat c \big\rangle$$
L_i the Lagrange score a candidate is ranked by; the platform picks arg max.
uvs_i unit value of a slot: the mean reputation b̄, discounted by the item's propaganda share and by the moderation penalty.
dbi_i diversity benefit: 1 minus the Hilbert overlap between the listener's embedding and the candidate's.
sai_i⁺ salience penalty: only salience beyond 0.22 is taxed, with weight μ.

Physical meaning. A constrained optimization: maximize value subject to a diversity constraint, written as a penalized objective.

Social meaning. The feed rewards broadcasters people trust, but pays extra for viewpoints outside the listener's current bubble. This is why B carries the contrast.

Context. In the model B (MORE) is the dominant path: main effect +0.6265, and the diversity-to-credibility weight is the top sensitivity driver (PRCC +0.77).

3. ERIC: reflect, invite, correct

$$m_i \;=\; \max\big(0,\; 1.4\,(p_i^{\mathrm{prop}} - \tau_{\mathrm{mod}})\big), \qquad p_i^{\mathrm{prop}}\text{: propaganda propensity},\;\tau_{\mathrm{mod}}\text{: moderation threshold}$$

Breakdown. If an item's propaganda propensity exceeds the threshold, ERIC applies a penalty proportional to the excess (slope 1.4). The penalty enters the slot value uvs_i and, in the full pipeline, the credibility mapping.

Physical meaning. A soft gating function: zero below threshold, linear beyond it.

Social meaning. Moderation as an interaction, not deletion: flagged items stay visible, ranked lower, and carry a reflection prompt back to the user.

Context. In the ablation, C alone is near zero (-0.0121); it matters through the B x C interaction (+0.3791).

4. Echo Meter and Algorithmic Cooling

$$e^{(t+1)} \;=\; 0.75\, e^{(t)} \;+\; 0.25\,(1-\mathrm{dbi}^{(t)}), \qquad e \;\leftarrow\; 0.76\, e \quad\text{if}\quad e \ge \theta_{\mathrm{cool}}$$

Breakdown. Echo accumulates toward sameness: three quarters of yesterday's echo plus one quarter of today's lack of diversity. Cooling multiplies by 0.76 once echo crosses the threshold, and the moderation factor (C) applies a further 0.85-0.94 damping in the simulator.

Physical meaning. Relaxation with active cooling: the echo state decays toward diversity, faster when hot.

Social meaning. The platform notices when you have been inside one bubble for a while, and gently widens the mix instead of telling you what you already agree with.

Context. Cooling is part of B; the mechanism check confirmed B x C lowers echo by -0.340 (SD .009).

5. The Zewail-inspired attention gate

$$R \;=\; \exp\!\big(-\,\Delta t \,/\, \tau\big), \qquad \mathrm{anchor} \;=\; 0.55\,c_i + 0.25\,g + 0.20\,\bar q + 0.20$$

Breakdown. R is the fraction of an evidence anchor still retained after a delay Δt, with dephasing time τ. The gate output g then weights the anchor: a listener inattentive for longer than τ retains almost nothing of the correction.

Physical meaning. Time-resolved coherence: the same idea as a pump-probe experiment, where the signal decays with the system's dephasing time.

Social meaning. Corrections have a half-life. If the platform does not re-anchor the audience quickly, the correction decays; attention is a resource with a time constant.

Context. The gate is open about 15% of the time in the baseline; the dephasing time is an assumption, not an empirical estimate, and the thesis says so.

6. GRM-IRT scoring

$$P(x \ge k \mid \theta) \;=\; \frac{1}{1 + \exp\!\big[-a\,(\theta - b_k)\big]}$$

Breakdown. θ is the latent trait (media literacy), b_k the difficulty of endorsing level k or above, a the item discrimination. Responses are simulated from this model to build the 35-item NMLS and 8-item MCS+PB batteries.

Physical meaning. A soft threshold crossing: the probability of endorsing a level rises sigmoidally as ability passes the difficulty.

Social meaning. Ratings are scored against item difficulties, not raw sums, so a "competence rating" means a position on a latent scale rather than a count of clicks.

Context. Reliability of the synthetic batteries: NMLS alpha .948, MCS alpha .738, test-retest ICC .953. These are properties of the generating model, not human validation.

7. DML and IPW debiasing

$$\hat\theta_{\mathrm{DML}} \;=\; \frac{\sum_i \big(Y_i - \hat m(X_i)\big)\big(Z_i - \hat e(X_i)\big)}{\sum_i \big(Z_i - \hat e(X_i)\big)^2}, \qquad w_i \;=\; \frac{Z_i}{\hat e(X_i)} + \frac{1-Z_i}{1-\hat e(X_i)}$$

Breakdown. DML partials out the nuisance functions m̂ (outcome model) and ê (propensity) before comparing treatment and control; IPW reweights every row by the inverse of its treatment probability.

Physical meaning. Orthogonalization: remove the predictable part, then measure what is left.

Social meaning. The 52 measured students were not randomly assigned; these procedures make the comparison as close to balanced as the observed covariates allow, and the residual imbalance (0.127 after IPW) is reported rather than hidden.

Context. The debiased rows define the manifold from which the 500 personas are drawn (condition number 4.82).

$$\text{Lagrange: } L_i = w_1 \mathrm{uvs}_i + w_2 \mathrm{dbi}_i - \mu\,\mathrm{sai}_i^{+} \qquad \text{Entropy: } e \leftarrow 0.76\,e \text{ if } e \ge \theta_c$$

Breakdown. Each session passes the state tensor through all eight; the ablation showed only three are outcome-active in this wiring: DeGroot (-0.233), Lagrange (-0.750), Hilbert (-0.453) when switched off. Laplace, Percolation, and Entropy leave the contrast unchanged, and Lyapunov is negligible; the Hamiltonian is a diagnostic and never enters the outcome.

Physical meaning. The loop is a concatenation of canonical dynamics: diffusion, consensus, contraction, spin-glass energy, embedding geometry, percolation, constrained choice, and cooling.

Social meaning. Each filter is one named social process: ideas spread, opinion conforms, attention contracts, energy concentrates, similarity attracts, rumor percolates, choice is constrained, and bubbles cool.

9. The joint sweep and PRCC sensitivity

$$r_{x_j} \;=\; \frac{\mathrm{Cov}\big(x_j,\, y \;\big|\; x_{-j}\big)} {\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}\big(x_j \mid x_{-j}\big)\;\mathrm{Var}\big(y \mid x_{-j}\big)}}$$

Breakdown. The 256-point sweep varies six mechanism parameters and five state-outcome weights including zero; the partial rank correlation of each input with the contrast, given the others, is reported. Top values: diversity-to-credibility +0.77, BRS-to-credibility +0.31, echo-to-credibility -0.27 (the sign is the weight's own sign).

Physical meaning. Local sensitivity: which dial, held fixed at its median, moves the answer most.

Social meaning. One declared assumption, the link between exposure diversity and perceived credibility, carries most of the result. The thesis names it; it does not hide it.

10. The four assignment designs

$$\widehat{ATE} \;=\; \frac{1}{n_1}\sum_{i: Z_i=1} Y_i \;-\; \frac{1}{n_0}\sum_{i: Z_i=0} Y_i$$

Breakdown. The same estimator is evaluated under four assignment rules: Z fixed within personas (all eight cells per persona), Z random per agent across 40 worlds, Z drawn from the potential-outcome matrix 400 times, and Z assigned per network cluster across 20 worlds. The design-based draw contains no interference, so comparing it with the agent-randomized design isolates interference.

Physical meaning. The same observable measured in four gauges.

Social meaning. A treatment effect that survives every assignment rule is a property of the design; one that moves with assignment is a property of the network. B survives; A does not.

Every equation on this page is implemented in the pipeline code and exercised in the interactive laboratory. The parameters are declared design inputs, not estimates, unless the text says otherwise.
