Everything the thesis did to check itself

Reliability, validity, sensitivity, robustness

Research quality is not one number; it is a set of distinct questions: is the measure stable (reliability), does it mean what it claims (validity), how much do the answers move with the assumptions (sensitivity), do they survive changes in design (robustness), and can anyone redo it (reproducibility)? This page answers all five for this thesis, in formal and in plain terms.

1. Reliability: is the measure stable?

Formal statement

Cronbach's alpha (internal consistency):

$$\alpha = \frac{k}{k-1}\left(1 - \frac{\sum_i \sigma_i^2}{\sigma_T^2}\right)$$

Model-based omega (one-factor, standardized loadings \(\lambda_i\), residuals \(\psi_i\)):

$$\omega = \frac{(\sum_i \lambda_i)^2}{(\sum_i \lambda_i)^2 + \sum_i \psi_i}$$

Cohen's kappa for coder agreement:

$$\kappa = \frac{p_o - p_e}{1 - p_e}$$

Plain terms

Alpha asks: do the items of one scale agree with each other? Omega asks the same question through a factor model instead of raw sums. Kappa asks: beyond chance, how much do two coders agree? All three are reported here, together with split-half and test-retest where available.

3. Sensitivity: how much do the answers move with the assumptions?

Formal statement

Partial rank correlation of input \(x_j\) with the contrast \(y\), given the other inputs:

$$r_{x_j} = \frac{\mathrm{Cov}(x_j, y \mid x_{-j})}{\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}(x_j \mid x_{-j})\;\mathrm{Var}(y \mid x_{-j})}}$$

Plain terms

Sensitivity asks: if I change one declared weight, how far does the result move? The sweep changes all eleven inputs together across 256 draws; the PRCC ranks which single input, everything else held at its typical value, moves the contrast most.

4. Robustness: does it survive changes in design?

Formal statement

The same estimator under four assignment rules:

$$\widehat{ATE} = \frac{1}{n_1}\sum_{i: Z_i=1} Y_i - \frac{1}{n_0}\sum_{i: Z_i=0} Y_i$$

with \(Z\) fixed within personas, randomized per agent, drawn from the potential-outcome matrix, or assigned per cluster.

Plain terms

Robustness asks: does the finding survive a different way of running the study? A result that survives every assignment rule is a property of the design; one that moves with assignment is a property of the network.

6. Inference quality: what the intervals mean

Formal statement

Frequentist: \(\bar d \pm 1.96\,s/\sqrt{n}\) over the 500 generated personas. Bayesian: a one-sample normal summary of the same paired differences. Both are re-expressions of the same simulated draws.

Plain terms

The narrow interval [0.779, 0.875] describes variation across generated personas, not uncertainty about humans. The thesis follows the ASA statement on p-values (Wasserstein & Lazar, 2016) and does not dress either summary up as independent evidence.
